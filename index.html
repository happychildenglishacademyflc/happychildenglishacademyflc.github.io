Our Story

Twelve years of turning shy hellos into confident conversations

Happy Child English Academy started in a single classroom in Kharuabandha with twenty students and one big idea: English sticks best when it's fun. Today, our teachers use phonics games, storytelling circles and real-world role-play to help children read, write and speak with genuine confidence.

Every lesson is built around a simple promise to parents — small classes, visible progress, and a teacher who knows your child's name and their favourite story.
